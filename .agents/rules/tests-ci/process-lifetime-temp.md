A temporary directory that lives until the process exits (a per-process cache, a replay workspace a caller may never dispose) is made with `processTempDir` from `src/core/platform/process-temp.ts`, never `mkdtempSync` plus an `exit` hook alone: a killed process (SIGKILL, a default SIGTERM or SIGINT, a test or command timeout, a cancelled CI job) never runs its `exit` hooks, and `baked-trace-cache-*` directories of about 1 GB each filled cloud and CI disks that way (#2967). `processTempDir` puts the process id in the name and its first call for a prefix removes the directories of processes that are gone; `tests/process-temp.test.ts` kills a child with SIGKILL and checks the next call frees its directory. A directory that only lives for one operation keeps its `try`/`finally` removal.
