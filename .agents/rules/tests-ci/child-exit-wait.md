A test that asserts what a child process does (its exit code, that it read end of file, that it did not prompt) waits for the child's `exit`, not for `close`, and gives the spawn a `timeout` a few seconds below the test's own limit. `close` also waits for every holder of the child's pipes, so a stalled pipe in a long `bun test` process ran a test out of its 30 s with nothing to tell a hung child from a held pipe; with the spawn timeout a hung child is killed and the assertion shows the signal (#3032).
