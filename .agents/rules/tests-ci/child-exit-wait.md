A test that asserts what a child process does (that it read end of file, what it printed) has the child print its answer and waits for the child's stdout to reach end of file, with its own deadline a few seconds below the test's limit; it does not wait for `exit` or `close`. On macOS a long `bun test` process sometimes never receives a short-lived child's exit: waiting for `close` (#3032) and then for `exit` with a 10 s spawn timeout (#3036) both ran out the 30 s limit while isolated reruns passed. When the deadline passes, the failure names the child's `ps` state (still running or already exited) so the next occurrence tells a stuck child from an undelivered exit.
