A claim on an issue is a comment that says "Claimed" (or an assignee, or an open pull request naming `#N`), and an issue that waits on the owner carries `needs-gulliby`, `deferred`, `idea` or `verification`. `scripts/pr-shepherd.ts` (`.github/workflows/pr-shepherd.yml`, every 2 h) lists every other open issue as unclaimed and every ready pull request outside the merge queue with conflicts, failing or hung checks or no auto-merge as stuck, in one tracking issue the shepherd loop works from; a new skip label or claim wording goes into its `SKIP_LABELS` or `CLAIM`, not into a line's habits.
