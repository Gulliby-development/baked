Whether the host would split a test task that keeps ending unfinished into slices of the prescribed rows its file still lacks is decided only by `rowSplitDecision` in `src/core/workflows/row-split.ts` (#2458), on the inputs `rowSplitRecord` reads from `suppliedRows` (never a second reader of row blocks) and the delivered file (`measureFileRows` and `rowPlaces`, the drift's pairing), recorded at every unfinished end through `recordRowSplit` in `scheduler.ts` in `row-splits/tasks/<task>.json`, replayed by `replayRowSplits` (`newRowSplits`), and through the `unfinished_row_split` reason (shadow). Its promotion (the next attempt takes the missing rows only, as chained modify-only row slices) is a pull request of its own after a bench round with no false decision, and adds its on case to `tests/workflow-scenario-unfinished-row-split.test.ts`; a new way a task ends unfinished goes through `recordUnfinished`, so it records the split too.
