A workflow step opened in `src/core/workflows/` goes under its stage step (`beginStep(runId, id, stepId)`), so the drive's catch, recovery and the run's end close it with the stage; a top-level step outside a stage closes itself on every exit path (try/catch or finally). No step stays `running` or `waiting` after its work ended (#1745, #1746); a task step's work runs inside `closingStep` in `scheduler.ts`, and an exit that blocks the task ends its step before the corrective is opened (#1805).
