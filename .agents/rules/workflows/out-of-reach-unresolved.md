Whether an unfinished task's unresolved item is out of its tools' reach (a path outside the workspace, or a file a later unfinished task writes) is decided only by `outOfReach` in `src/core/workflows/out-of-reach.ts`, and the scheduler's `hostFinishOutOfReach` is the one place that finishes such a task, after `hostFinishAfterStop` and before any restart, on both the single-writer and the batch-worker path (#3021). A new kind of step no task tool can take adds its reason there with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-unfinished-out-of-reach.test.ts`; an item in the task's own scope must keep returning null so the run still stops on a real gap.
