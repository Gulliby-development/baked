Whether an analysis return is answered by an amendment that keeps the plan and tasks, instead of re-running specify, plan and analyze, is decided only by `amendableReturn` and `amendKeepsPlan` in `src/core/workflows/derivation-audit.ts` (policy `derivationAmend`, #2219), and the decision is recorded under `spec-repairs/derivation-amend-N.json`. Today only `DERIVE-` findings may be amended in place; any other finding kind (ambiguity, coverage, a plan gap) is structural and keeps the full return. A new analysis finding kind states whether it may be amended in place: it joins `amendableReturn` with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-derivation-amend.test.ts`, including the fallback when the amendment changes a task's criterion list.
