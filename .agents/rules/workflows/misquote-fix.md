What corrects a misquoted test when the host does not (`misquoteFix`, #2047/#2058) is decided by `misquoteFallback` in `src/core/workflows/misquote-fix.ts` and recorded as the `fallback` of `conflicts/misquote/TASK.json`; a new fallback (another bounded continuation, another settle kind) adds its value there and its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-misquote-host-fix.test.ts`, never a branch of its own in `settleContestedRows`. A settle that changes one assertion (audited rows, request wins, #2181) decides its continuation by `settleFallback` through `settleContinue` in `src/core/workflows/scheduler.ts`, recorded as `conflicts/misquote/TASK-SETTLE.json`; a new settle kind adds its `SettleKind` value and its case to that test file, and a task path that settles a misquote (best-of-N candidates included) passes a `MisquoteFixContext`.
