A fixer that formats a range of lines (a `range` row of `FILE_FORMATTERS` in `src/core/workflows/format-written.ts`) is held to its range by the host, never trusted to stay in it: its check carries the range as `fixLines`, and both fixer sites (the checks stage's fixer pass in `scheduler.ts` and `apply_fix` in `agent-executor.ts`) put back its changes outside the range through `putBackOutsideRange` in `src/core/workflows/range-fixer-scope.ts`, kept only when the check still passes, and record it with `recordPutBack`. A new range row adds a sample and a case to `tests/workflow-scenario-range-fixer-scope.test.ts`, whose table fails for a range formatter without one.
