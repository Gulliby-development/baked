A field of a task attempt's input that carries earlier work into a repeated, restarted or corrective attempt (`earlierEdits`, `earlierReads`, `previousAttempt`, `recurringFindings`, `correctionFocus`, `battery`, `misquoteContinue`, `failedFixers`) is added to the `attempt-context` keys in `src/core/workflows/scheduler.ts`, so `attempts/ID/context.json` records it, and to the attempt-context paragraph of `docs/workflows/runtime.md`; its scenario test asserts the saved context equals what was sent, as `tests/workflow-scenario-recurring-symbol.test.ts` does (#1774).
