A task-level corrective's write set contains the files its failed non-test check names in the failing task's own files, also while a later slice of a split test file is still to run, and a diagnostics-only corrective writes only the failing task's own files (`diagnosedFiles`, `diagnosticsOnly` and `ownScope` in `src/core/workflows/corrective-scope.ts`, read by `appendCorrections`); a precondition a model cannot meet (a diagnosed file outside the writes) is decided at creation and before each session by `scopeRecord`, recorded under `scope/tasks/` for `replayCorrectiveScopes`, and refused through the `corrective_scope` stop reason (shadow until promoted), never after the sessions. A corrective never waits for a task that depends on a task it corrects (`waitsOnRepaired` in `task-graph.ts`). A new kind of task-level corrective, a new path that builds one, or a new hold in `readyTasks` adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-corrective-sliced-test.test.ts` (and a toolchain row to its `TOOLCHAINS` table, or a stack to the gate-matrix cell "sliced-test-diagnostics-corrective"), which fails when a corrective's writes lack a file its own diagnostics name or when an open corrective holds a task its writes do not touch (#2094). An open corrective's hold reads its narrowed writes through `repairHolds` in `scheduler.ts` (passed to `readyTasks` and `failureOwner`), never its full write scope until it widens, so a hold reaches only tasks that read the files its findings point at (#2109). A corrective that blocks is decided by `correctiveDeferDecision` (`src/core/workflows/corrective-defer.ts`) in `parkBlockedCorrective`, on the single-writer and the batch path alike, and recorded under `blocks/correctives/` for `replayCorrectiveDefers`; a parked corrective keeps its hold through the `parked` argument of `readyTasks`, and a new place that ends the drive on a corrective's stop goes through `parkBlockedCorrective` and adds its case to the sliced-test scenario file. A corrective's evidence names the files it must change, and the host decides owner, widen or park in one function: every origin of a corrective (a check, a review, a probe, a battery, a worker's unresolved report) names its files through `scopeRecord` (`findingOrigin`, `evidencePaths`, `stackFrames` in `corrective-scope.ts`), the decision over a named file outside the writes is `unownedRule` and `hostDecision` only, reached through `unownedFile` in `scheduler.ts` before any restart or session on the single-writer and the batch path, recorded under `scope/tasks/` for `replayCorrectiveScopes` (`newUnownedDecisions`) and enforced only through the `corrective_unowned_file` stop reason (shadow by default); a widened write goes through a `host-widen` correction link that acceptance checks with `widenAuthorized`, and a precondition no model session can meet refuses at creation or before the session, never after it. A new origin of a corrective, or a new path that opens or restarts one, adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-corrective-unowned-file.test.ts` (and a stack frame to `STACK_FRAMES` in the gate-matrix cell "corrective-unowned-file" for a new runtime's frame format), which fails when a corrective's finding names a path outside its writes and no owner, widen or park decision is recorded (#2133).
