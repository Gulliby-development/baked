A limit that refuses a correction after its pass recorded the findings (`refuse` in `appendCorrections`, a v2 loop bound) names those findings in its repair block as `stranded` with the pass that raised them, and a person's grant hands them to the next pass of that kind, which retires those it does not raise again through `retireStranded` (`src/core/workflows/scheduler.ts`), never a second rule of its own. A finding that the acceptance predicate counts must have a way to `resolved` or `refuted` after every remedy a stop offers; a new refusal after `recordFindings` passes `stranded` too, with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-stranded-findings.test.ts` (#2878).
