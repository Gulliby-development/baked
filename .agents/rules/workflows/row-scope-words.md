A new phrasing joins `scopeRowRanges` with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-feature-row-split.test.ts`, and every reader of a feature's rows (`featureRowFiles`, `mergedPartRows`, `rowOwnerProblems`) goes through `featureRowSplit`, never the scope text itself.
