A close or dispose that stops child processes resolves only once they have exited, the ones still starting too, and a worker that owns them awaits that stop before it exits: `LspBackend.dispose` and `StandaloneLspClient.stop` wait for the exit, the intelligence worker awaits `router.dispose()`, `IntelligenceClient.close()` waits for its worker to exit (`WorkerClient.disposeAndWait`), and a recycled worker is retired through its dispose handler, never a bare `terminate()` (`WorkerClient.retire`). A worker gone before it saw its child exit leaves the child unreaped, and a bare terminate leaves every server it ran. An owner that starts children through a handshake (an MCP stdio transport, a language server) tracks them while they start and stops them on dispose and on a handshake timeout (`MCPManager.startingTransports`). `tests/lsp-starting-server-stop.test.ts` and `tests/mcp-starting-server-stop.test.ts` fail when a starting server outlives its owner (#2800).
