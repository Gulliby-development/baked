A linter, formatter or type checker a check may be named after goes into `STATIC_TOOLS` (`src/core/workflows/static-tools.ts`, a `LanguageRecord` registered in `tests/language-parity.test.ts`) under its language, with its `staticTools` golden in the language's fixture: `isStaticCheck`, `isWholeUnitCheck`, `isTestCheck`, `styleClaimShown` and the CI step kinds of `detectGates` all read it, so never add a tool name to one of their regular expressions instead (#2110). Mark `wholeUnit` a tool that resolves the whole project (imports, classes, the compiled program) and `prefix` one whose build-tool tasks start with its name (`spotlessCheck`).
