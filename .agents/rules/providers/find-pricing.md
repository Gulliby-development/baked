A model's price comes from `findPricing` in `src/core/llm/pricing.ts`, which every cost figure (status bar, `baked usage`, run usage, estimates, cost caps) reads. A table entry prices only the model it names and that model's dated or tagged releases (`namesModel`): a variant (`-pro`, `-nano`, `-mini`, `-fast`, `-codex` and the other words of `VARIANT_WORD`) or a later version (`claude-opus-4.6` after `claude-opus-4`) gets its own entry or a family price marked `approximate`, never the base's price as `table`. A new entry for a variant, or a new variant word, adds its case to `tests/pricing-variants.test.ts`.
