A provider adapter whose turn finishes, and is billed, without an answer it can use (no final message, a `tool-calls` finish with no tool call, a protocol reply missing what the call needs) throws `ProviderAnswerError` (`src/core/llm/provider-answer.ts`) with the usage the turn reported and what its stream carried, never a plain `Error`. The scheduler climbs the recovery ladder on it and the executor charges its tokens, where a plain `Error` was filed as `provider_unavailable` at 0 tokens; the fault matrix's `lost-answer` profile covers every adapter (#1957).
