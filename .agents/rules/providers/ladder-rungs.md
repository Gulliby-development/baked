A stalled model call (no first response, or a stream that ended without a finish) recovers through the rungs of the recovery ladder in `src/core/workflows/stall-ladder.ts` (`LADDER_RUNGS`, `nextRung`), not through another retry branch in `scheduler.call` (#1877). A new rung says when it changes something for the call, keeps what earlier rungs changed, gets its `rungText` and a case in `tests/workflow-stall-ladder.test.ts`; a knob a provider adapter reads per call travels in provider options (`withCodexRecovery`), and the replay hook `replayStallLadder` decides it again on the corpus. A new default of the `next-model` rung's fallback list (`siblingFallbacks` in `stall-siblings.ts`) adds a profile to the fault matrix with the class it expects, and records its inputs so the replay takes the same model (#2271). A replay reader that decides "stalled" from a call's timing reads `failure` too (`isStall` in `provider-health.ts` is the one classifier the run, the timing and the replay share): a call that ended in a provider error is the retry's, never a stall (#2820).
