Subscription usage limits come from `LIMIT_READERS` in `src/core/llm/usage-limits.ts`, which `GET /v1/providers/limits`, `baked limits`, `/limits` and the desktop's Subscription limits panel render as they are. A provider that gains a subscription limit adds a reader there and its parse case to `tests/usage-limits.test.ts` (whose first test lists the readers), and reads it the way the provider offers without reading a CLI's own tokens; a report a provider only sends during a turn is recorded from its provider through `usage-limits-record.ts`, which imports no provider (#2300).
