The adaptive first-response deadline of a silent call (`FirstByteHistory.deadline` in `src/core/llm/first-byte-history.ts`, in the provider-timers hot module) scales with the call's size: every answer keeps its prompt size (`promptChars` in its `calls` timing), and a call larger than its shape's answers gets their slowest pace per character times its own size. A heuristic deadline that ran out unanswered gives the calls of the shape the ceiling before the ladder repeats the input, until a call at least as large answers: a retry attempt opens with short calls of the same shape, and an answer clears only what its size proves (#2193). A new call kind whose duration grows with an input other than its prompt adds that unit to the timing and the history, a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-analysis-deadline.test.ts`, and replays through `replayFirstBytes` (#2041). The deadline's inputs are recorded in the call's timing (`deadlineInputs`, `firstByteInputs`), because the shared history and the process's seed are not in the run's database; a new input of the deadline joins `FirstByteInputs` (#2283).
