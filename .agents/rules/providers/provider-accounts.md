Several accounts of one provider (#2303) live in the provider layer only. Which account a project uses is decided only by `activeProviderAccount` in `src/core/llm/provider-accounts.ts`, from the global config and never from a project's own `.baked/config.json`, and every process BAKED starts for a provider CLI on an account's behalf (model turns, login, status, logout) gets its environment from `providerAccountEnv` in `src/core/llm/providers/account-env.ts`; workflows, agents and front ends never name an account home or a credential variable, and the surfaces render `providerAccountsView` (`src/core/llm/account-overview.ts`). A provider that gains accounts adds itself to `ACCOUNT_PROVIDERS` and its home and environment row to `ACCOUNT_ENV`, passes the account environment to every spawn of its CLI, and keys any process or session it reuses by account so a change applies to the next turn; `tests/provider-accounts.test.ts` fails on an account provider without its `ACCOUNT_ENV` row. Nothing moves a project to another account on its own: an automatic switch on exhaustion is a host decision under the stop-reason and replay rules above, opt-in per project and limited to the accounts assigned to it.
