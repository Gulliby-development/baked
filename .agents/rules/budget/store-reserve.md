Every model attempt (`store.reserve` with a model) writes `attempts/ID/usage.json` before `finishAttempt`, in the same transaction: the run's estimated cost, the dollar cap, `baked workflow usage`, `workflow compare`, the bench reports and `chainCosts` price an attempt only from that record, and a model attempt without one counts its tokens but not its dollars. A model call the executor makes outside the step loop (the format correction, `correctionUsage` in `src/core/workflows/agent-executor.ts`) records its calls as steps there (#2777); `tests/workflow-prose-end.test.ts` fails when it does not.
