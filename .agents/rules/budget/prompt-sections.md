What an attempt's first prompt holds is recorded as `prompt.sections` in `attempts/ID/usage.json`, split by `contextPieces` and `promptSections` in `src/core/workflows/prompt-sections.ts` (#2597). A new prompt section (a part of `buildWorkflowContext`, a document or a key of the attempt's input) is named in `prompt.sections`, never left in `framing` or `envelope`, and `tests/workflow-scenario-prompt-sections.test.ts` asserts that the listed sections sum to `firstBytes`, so a section left out fails the test. The large-prompt notice (`largePrompt`, `attempt.large_prompt`) only records: it never changes what an attempt is sent or whether it runs.
