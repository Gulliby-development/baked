A price fallback for a model without paid samples of its own (`forecastRate`, `rateOf` in `plan-cost.ts`, `taskCallRate`, the pre-run estimate) takes its token mix from `providerTaskMix` (`src/core/workflows/stage-price.ts`) and measures a mix only from attempts of the model's own provider family (`providerFamily`); no code borrows another provider's measured mix or prices a provider that pays for cache writes at a mix without a cache-write share. A new provider adapter adds its family to `providerFamily` and, when it pays for cache writes, its own mixes with their write share, measured from recorded attempts, with a backtest case in `tests/forecast-replay-corpus-provider-mix.test.ts`. The per-provider forecast ships in shadow (`providerTokenMix`, recorded as `providerMix` in the forecast record) until a bench round shows it closer to what was paid (#2963).
