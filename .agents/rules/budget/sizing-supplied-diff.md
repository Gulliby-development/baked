Whether a task is priced from the lines the request supplies (`sizing.suppliedDiff`, #2002) is decided by one function, `suppliedTaskPrice` in `src/core/workflows/sizing.ts`: `sizePlan` takes that price, its `basis` and its split from it and from nowhere else. A new kind of supplied block is read in `suppliedLines`, which reuses the Markdown `fences` of `doc-examples.ts` and the diff reader `changedLines` of `call-context.ts` rather than a parser of its own, with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-supplied-diff-sizing.test.ts`.
