A sizing rule built on evidence of how cheap a feature can be (fewer paths than its pool, `fewPathDetail` in `src/core/workflows/initiatives/estimate.ts`) moves the low end of its range only. The high end stays the pool's, or the measured actual-to-low ratios' bound (#1982) where that is narrower. Its point forecast (`expectedTokens`, which the accepted-feature ratio is measured against) stays the middle of the scaled range, so widening the high end does not loosen a calibration or the share gate (R66 nodepluck's true park, `tests/forecast-replay-corpus.test.ts`). Scaling the pool's upper quartile along with the low end put the high end at about twice the low end, under every measured overrun, and put 12 of the 22 share-priced features of rounds 95 to 100 above their range (#2941). A new rule that scales a range adds the corpus share above the range, as well as the share below it, to its backtest.
