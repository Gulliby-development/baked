A dollar price that splits a run's tokens across its stages before the run measured them (an initiative feature's `costUsd` in `priceFeatureNeeds`, and any later price of planned stages) weights each stage by what runs of the same route spent on it: `routedStageWeights` (`src/core/workflows/forecast-price.ts`) takes the mean tokens of finished runs that ran the stage on its routed model, read from price samples that carry their `runId` (`forecastSamples`), and never a stage weight measured on runs of another route or provider. A stage priced at its model's card but weighted by another route's share priced R103's Claude features, with Opus-tier planning and Sonnet-tier tasks, at $2.39 per Mtok against $3.32 to $3.90 paid (#2963). A new surface that prices planned stages calls the helper and records its price beside the current one under `routedStageShare` (`off`, `shadow` by default, `on`) until a bench round promotes it; a trace replay that routes features restores the runner's workflow stage routes and `specifyOnSonnet` from the trace (`recordedRouting` in `src/core/workflows/trace.ts`). `tests/forecast-replay-corpus-routed-share.test.ts` replays the Claude-routed starts of R103 on and fails when a routed price lands more than 5% under what its feature paid.
