An attempt's all-token cap (cache reads included) is computed only by `allTokensCapFor` in `src/core/workflows/step-budget.ts`, which the step controller and `attemptCapFault` both call; a new input to it is added there and to `allTokensInputs` in `usage.json`, never as a second factor at a call site (#2650). Its floor never sits below the step limit at the attempt's average request, so a limit that ends attempts by tokens never makes the step limit unreachable; `tests/workflow-step-budget.test.ts` (the R88 md-site replay) fails when it does.
