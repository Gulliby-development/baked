A started feature run's remaining mandatory stages (its review and convergence after the last task) are reserved ahead of the waiting features' low estimates only in `stageReserveDecision` (`src/core/workflows/initiatives/growth.ts`, bounded by the initiative's dollar cap with its tolerance), priced only by `stageReserve` (`src/core/workflows/stage-reserve.ts`, on the cost forecast's `verificationStages`), and asked only through the store's `reserveStages` hook when a model call would stop on the run's cost ceiling (installed by `NativeWorkflowService`, answered by `InitiativeService.reserveStages`), never as a second dollar room beside `costGrowthLimit` (#2241, R71 xmlvet get-command). It is the `stage-reserve` row of `HOST_POOL_GROWTHS`: `workflows.stageReserve` defaults to `shadow`, its opt-in `on` grows the bound, and making `on` the default is a pull request of its own. A shadow record taken inside a transaction that the stop then rolls back is written through `store.afterTransaction`, never emitted inside it, or it vanishes with the refused call. A cost block that can price what finishes the run names it on its `extend` remedy (`suggestedUsd` from `stageSuggestedUsd`); a new case goes in `tests/workflow-scenario-started-run-stage-reserve.test.ts` and the stage reserve invariants in `tests/workflow-accounting-invariants.test.ts`. The state a cost refusal leaves a run in is decided in one place, the scheduler's fault handler, from the fault's recovery through `costRefusalWait` (`src/core/workflows/stop-reasons.ts`, the `cost_refusal_wait` reason, shadow until promoted), never per stage; a new stage after the tasks or a new cost refusal path reaches it through the store's `cost` fault with its `stages`, and an initiative counts a dependant as stalled only through `stalledUpstream` (`initiatives/service.ts`), with a case in that scenario test, which fails when a refusal at review records no would-wait (#2249).
