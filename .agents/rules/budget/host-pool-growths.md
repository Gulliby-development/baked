A pool or cap growth the host makes, or would make, on its own is bounded by another cap the person set and is recorded with its inputs. Each such growth registers its reason in `HOST_POOL_GROWTHS` (`src/core/workflows/initiatives/token-pool.ts`, in the cap-growth hot module) with the cap that bounds it, ships in shadow, and adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-token-pool.test.ts`, which fails on a recorded `decision.would_extend` whose reason is missing there, and its invariant to `tests/workflow-accounting-invariants.test.ts`; growing for real is a pull request of its own. Today that is the shared time's extension inside the token pool and the dollar cap (`timePoolExtensionDecision`, `workflows.timePoolGrowth`, shadow only, replayed by `replayTimePoolGrowth`, #2346) and the token pool's extension inside the dollar cap (`poolExtensionDecision`, `workflows.extendWithinDollars`, opt-in `on` since #2060, which only applies the same decision as a `budget.extended` event `by: "host"`): the park's `poolGrowth` and the event's `inputs` replay through `replayPoolGrowth` (`newPoolGrowth`). The same opt-in also acts at plan time (#2488): a started feature run whose own plan sizing needs more than its ceiling asks its initiative before its first task (`planShareDecision`, the `plan-share` row, replayed by `replayPlanShare` with `newPlanShare`), and a new place where a run learns it needs more than its share asks the initiative the same way, never the model. A growth that cannot act until a measurement exists says so where the person looks: `extendWithinDollars: on` waits for the first accepted feature's dollar forecast, so a first-wave park records `poolGrowth.hold: "no measurement"` (`poolGrowthHold`) and its message and the forecast notice name the manual extend (#2471); a new hold of a host growth gets the same record, text and case in that scenario test, and is decided again when the measurement it waits for exists (the first accepted feature re-asks a held park and a held plan-share gate, #2523), never only once per run. A host growth decided on plan charges (a park's committed tokens, the plan-time share gate's need and `heldTokens`) reads them at the plan point `planChargeTokens` charges before a ratio is measured (the high end, or the expected need with `workflows.planChargeExpected: on`, #2534), never at a point of its own: R83 pnmforge's +2.7M extension and geometry's 30-minute park rested on high-end charges of 18.98M for 4.7M used. A bound derived from a plan charge is capped by what the pool and the ceiling can hold (`chargeHold` in `growth.ts`, #2280), and `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-token-pool.test.ts` fails on a case where a charge above the pool becomes a bound; a bound still above them is recorded by the `initiative-pool-growth` shadow reason (`poolBoundDecision`, replayed by `replayPoolBounds`). An initiative run's token growth room is never null: a path that derives a run's token bound from the initiative goes through `growthLimit` and stays at or under the pool left (an initiative's last open feature has no waiting feature, and a null room took the base ceilings, #2294), with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-guard-growth.test.ts`. Every block on a shared budget names the amounts that answer it and puts them on its `extend` remedy (`suggestedTokens`, `suggestedUsd` in `remedies-model.ts`), so the answer is one command, key or click; a new budget block does the same, with a case in that scenario test (#2042). A token pool a block suggests goes through `affordableTokens` (`token-pool.ts`): held to what the dollar cap pays for, or with the cap the larger pool needs on the same remedy, and its message names the same amount (#2367). Every budget dimension of an initiative's shared pool (tokens, calls, time, dollars) has a measured rate, a forecast notice and a suggested amount on its `extend` remedy (`budget.pool`, `budget.calls`, `budget.time`, `budget.cost`; `suggestedTokens`, `suggestedCalls`, `suggestedTimeMs`, `suggestedUsd`), and a feature run blocked on its active deadline offers `extend` with its time before `resume`. `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-time-pool.test.ts` fails when a limit of the pool lacks one of the three (#2336, calls #2345). A measured rate excludes time the provider did not answer: a call that ends with no output and no tool call (deadline, ladder, watchdog, cancel) gives its whole interval back to active time (`ExecutionBudgetClock.pending` settled `false` after `true`), and an answered call's wait before its first response is recorded as `silentMs` on its call timing and attempt usage record. Only the stall part of that wait is time the provider did not answer: what passes the deadline the call's shape had from its healthy answers (`firstByteDeadline` on its `deadlineInputs`, else its `limitMs`), never less than the 3 minute adaptive floor (`callStallMs` in `src/core/workflows/clean-pace.ts`, recorded as `stallMs` beside `silentMs`, #2424), because Codex and Claude Code buffer their answer and their wait is model time (R62 stepvet's and R77 pairwick's clean rates beat the history and their real pace when the whole wait was left out). A pace that leaves the stalls out is recorded in shadow beside the deciding one (`cleanPace` in `budget.time`, the time notice and sizing reports, with `silentMs` and `stallMs`, replayed by `replayActiveTimes` with the `cleanPaceChanges` counter; a record without `stallMs` is read again from its calls' timings) until a pull request of its own makes it decide; a new reader of the waits reads `stallMs`, never `silentMs`, for a pace, and keeps the R62 stepvet case in `tests/forecast-replay-corpus.test.ts` (the clean pace keeps the initiative's 71k). A suggested amount is held to what its pool can grant, and a larger one names the pool's extend first (`heldRunTime` for a feature run's `suggestedTimeMs`, `affordableTokens` for tokens) (#2414).
