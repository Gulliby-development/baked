Every shared budget dimension the start gate splits (tokens, dollars, active time, calls) has a low-estimate start test: the share left after the other waiting features' lows against the starting feature's own low, never only a floor such as `MIN_SHARE`. For active time it is only `timeShortfall` in `src/core/workflows/initiatives/service.ts` (the `initiative-time-share` reason, shadow-only, #2413), the token lows at `budget.time.pace`; R76 schemamask started three features on 2 to 4 minutes against lows of 9 to 30 and each waited 16 to 26 minutes for a person. Its promotion to `enforce` waits for the pace to leave out silent provider time (#2414) and cites two bench rounds whose replayed starts (`timeWouldStop` in `replayInitiativeStarts`) hold no would-stop for a feature that finished inside its deadline.
