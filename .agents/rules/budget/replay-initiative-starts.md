A replay that rewinds a bench trace to a moment (`replayInitiativeStarts`, `replayRun`) restores every input the replayed decision reads as it stood then, never the trace's final state: each run's tokens, dollars, agent calls, active time and policy (`rewindRunBudget`, `runActiveAt` and `runPolicyAt` in `src/core/workflows/trace.ts`) and the initiative's budget before a later extension. An attempt's reservation is one of those inputs: a check of its largest reservation is replayed at the raise that reached it (the last `budget.updated` while it ran, whose total the other open attempts are scaled down to), never at its start with the policy of then (R70 evalpin suite-runner's reviews, `replayRun`). A new stored total or limit that a decision reads joins `rewindRunBudget`, and the replay's case compares the rewound inputs with the decision's recorded ones (R60 wheeltags' share gates, #2061): replayed on final run costs and grown caps, R60 parked four starts on $0.00, and R59 and R62 showed dollar-share would-stops the rounds never recorded. A run counter left final is the same defect: with final active time and calls, R76 schemamask parked two starts on 0 ms of shared time (#2417). The history stores are such an input: each store the policy names replays from its own corpus trace, matched in `corpusHistoryTrace` alone, by file name or else by the project folder the round's traces recorded (a short bench folder such as `R85/csp`, from R81 on) or the app name it abbreviates, never by an alias table to keep by hand; a store the corpus holds that lands in `missingHistory` is that defect: from R81 to R92 none of the short folders mapped, and R95 gapmeet priced from 10 of its 32 stores (#2894).
