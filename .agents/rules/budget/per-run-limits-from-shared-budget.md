A per-run limit derived from an initiative's shared budget (a feature run's dollar and token growth bounds, its calls and active time) is refreshed for open runs in `refreshedGrowth` (`src/core/workflows/initiatives/growth.ts`, in the cap-growth hot module), which the drive calls before each run is driven and a run's active-time deadline calls before it stops (`growLimits`), and never only at the feature's start; the run's drive adopts the stored limits (`adoptStoredLimits`). A limit the initiative can raise while a drive runs is adopted through one function that also re-arms its timer (`adoptLimits` in `scheduler.ts`), and a timer that fires decides on the adopted limit and the time left, never on whether its own look raised it (#2216); a new such limit adds a case to `tests/workflow-scenario-active-deadline-rearm.test.ts` or the guard-growth scenario test. A host resume of a budget block (a run blocked `budget_exhausted` on its own active time, tokens or calls whose stored limits grew past its usage) is decided in one function with its inputs recorded, `storedLimitResume` in `src/core/workflows/stop-reasons.ts` over the block's `limit-blocks/` record, reached only through `noteStoredLimitResume` (the drive's block, the initiative's driver round), ships in shadow (`host-resume-on-stored-limit`, `decision.would_resume`), and a new place that looks at a blocked run again, or a new limit a block can hold, adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-active-deadline-rearm.test.ts`, which fails when a raised limit records no would-resume or one that stays at its usage records one (#2226). The room for calls and active time counts another open run at its use, not its limit (`openGuardRoom`). A new limit of that kind joins `refreshedGrowth` with its room and a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-growth-refresh.test.ts` or `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-guard-growth.test.ts` (#1931, #1958). A new path that raises a feature run's token or dollar limit reads the initiative's hold for the run (`chargeHold` for a bound from its plan charge, `WorkflowStore.tokenHold` for the run's own token growth in `budget.ts`: `auto-growth`, `planning-growth`, `auto-extend`, the planning floor) and adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-growth-refresh.test.ts`: R79 deltaglue's chunks auto-extended to 12.0M on its dollar bound with 1.86M of the 12.0M pool used by the others (#2468). The hold counts every other run of the initiative at what it takes from the pool, an open run at its granted limit or its use if more, a finished one at its use (`poolTokenHold` in `growth.ts`), never at its use alone, and never the run's own limit: two runs that extend at about the same time each took the pool less the others' use, 18M of a 12M pool (#2481); a new hold of a run's growth reserves the others' granted limits the same way and keeps the two-run case in that scenario test and the granted-limits invariant in `tests/workflow-accounting-invariants.test.ts`. A growth of the run's own token budget that waits for progress (`planning-growth`, `auto-extend` in `budget.ts`) counts the tokens open attempts used since its last growth as progress (`inFlightSince`; for `planning-growth` only when more than one open attempt spent them, so one attempt alone still stops on its budget, #1298), only while the step fits under its hard ceiling, the pool hold and what the dollar cap pays for, and records them (`openAttemptTokens`, `inFlightTokens`, `affordableTokens`); a new progress-gated growth does the same, with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-extend-in-flight-writers.test.ts` (#2590, #2607).
