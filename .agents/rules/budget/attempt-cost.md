An attempt's dollar cost is priced only by `attemptCost` (`src/core/workflows/usage.ts`), which every cost reader (the run's cost and dollar cap, `runUsage`, the stage rates, the trace) goes through. A step charged at an estimate (an output-cap stop, a lost answer) is a step of `usage.json`, and `estimatedUsage` sums every step including it, so a price adds only the part of `estimatedUsage` the steps do not carry; a new estimate written to the record follows the same rule, with a case in `tests/workflow-attempt-usage.test.ts` that prices it once (#2669).
