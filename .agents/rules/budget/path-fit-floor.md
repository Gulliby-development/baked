A size pool's path fit (`placeBy` in `src/core/workflows/initiatives/estimate.ts`) floors a feature at `fitFloor`: the pool's cheapest sample that names paths, times the square root of the feature's share of that sample's paths when it has fewer, never the cheapest whole sample alone, which priced R91 stopcut's one- and two-path features at a many-path feature's cost (#2895). A change to that floor backtests the corpus range shares (`tests/forecast-replay-corpus-few-paths.test.ts`) both ways, below and above: a floor that lowers the whole range, such as the lowest per-path rate times the paths, also lowers the high end the measured ratios bound, and that one put six more large features above their range.
