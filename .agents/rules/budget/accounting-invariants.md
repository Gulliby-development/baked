A pull request that adds an accounting quantity (a token, cost, reservation or forecast figure of a run) or a scheduler command adds its invariant to `tests/workflow-accounting-invariants.test.ts` or its command to the model in `tests/workflow-scheduler-model.test.ts` in the same pull request. What an initiative's shared pool holds for an open run (`committed`, `committedUsd`) is one rule per budget, counted in `InitiativeService.view`: a new charge for an open run (today the limit, or with `initiative-plan-charge` the high end of its sized plan's need at the measured ratio, never below its use, `planCharge` in `initiatives/token-pool.ts`, #2100; in dollars the same tokens at the initiative's task-call rate, never below spent plus reserved, `planChargeUsd`, #2124: a budget without a measured ratio prices the charge at the rate of the calls that scale with it rather than keeping the limit) replaces that rule both ways rather than adding a second reserve beside it, ships in shadow with a `decision.would_release` record, and adds its case to the plan charge invariants in `tests/workflow-accounting-invariants.test.ts` (never below what the runs used, freed tokens counted once) and a backtest row in `tests/forecast-replay-corpus.test.ts` (`planCharge` on each rewound start). How much an open run's plan is held at is decided only in `planChargeTokens` (the dollar side prices its tokens): an exception for a kind of plan (today, unmeasured, a plan whose low end is above its run's limit is held at its expected need, #2253) goes there, is backtested on every plan the corpus prices above its limit, and keeps the replay case in `tests/forecast-replay-corpus.test.ts` that R60 wheeltags parks no start and R71 xmlvet's canonical still parks. Which plan point it charges before a plan-to-actual ratio is measured is its `point` (`PlanChargePoint`, `initiatives/token-pool.ts`, in the cap-growth hot module): today the high end, or with `workflows.planChargeExpected: on` (opt-in; `shadow`, the default, records the expected-point charge as `budget.planCharge.expected` and a `decision.would_release` with `point: "expected"` at a share gate it would start, #2534) the plan's expected need, never below what the run used; a measured ratio, and a plan without expected tasks, keep the high end. A change of that point says here which point it charges, changes `point` rather than a reader (the share gate's committed tokens, the plan-share gate's need and `heldTokens`, `chargeHold` all read `chargeTokens`), is replayed with `replayInitiativeStarts`' `planChargeExpected` on the corpus, and keeps the replay cases on the R82 tinyflate and R83 pnmforge numbers in `tests/workflow-scenario-initiative-token-pool.test.ts` (21.5M of a 19.6M pool at the high end; geometry's park and the +2.7M extension rested on 18.98M of charges for 4.7M used).
