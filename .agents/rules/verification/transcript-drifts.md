Whether a delivered document keeps a `$` command transcript the request says it must hold exactly is decided only by `transcriptDrifts` in `src/core/workflows/transcript-drift.ts` (#2648), from the request's blocks read through `fences`, called at the checks stage of both workflow versions next to `suppliedDriftWouldStop` and recorded through the `transcript_drift` reason (shadow). A new wording of exactness or of a document joins `EXACT` or `DOCUMENT` there, and a new kind of request text a delivered file must hold (another part of a document than a whole file, a section, a diff, a row or a transcript) gets its own drift check beside these, with its stop reason in shadow, a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-transcript-drift.test.ts` or its own scenario test, and a gate-matrix cell.
