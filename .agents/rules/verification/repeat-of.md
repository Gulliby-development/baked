A finding chain (a `repeatOf` chain on one file and symbol, `src/core/workflows/finding-chain.ts`) escalates at one threshold, `CHAIN_REWRITE_AT`; never compare a chain's length with a number written elsewhere. The rewrite corrective's battery travels as the `battery` attempt-context key and the host's battery check before a review reads the same chain (`batteryOf`), so the worker and the host hold the same cases. Both decisions, and the rewrite's sibling probe (`chainFamily`, `familyDecision`, #1969), record their inputs under `chains/` (kind `finding-chain`, kept in bench traces) and replay in `replayFindingChains`. A chain record counts the earlier rewrites of its place (`rewrites`, `withRewrites`, #2568): the second rewrite probes whatever `chainProbe` says and carries the reviewer's `design` (`rewriteDesign`), and a finding after `CHAIN_REWRITE_REPEAT_AT` rewrites records the `chain_rewrite_repeat` would-stop; a new corrective origin that rewrites a symbol reads `rewrites` from the chain record, never a count of its own, adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts` and its column to `replayFindingChains` (the `rewrite_repeat` boundary). A new corrective origin, or a new path that opens a corrective for a review or convergence finding, adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts`, which fails when the second link of a chain is not a rewrite with its battery or when a broken battery case reaches a review call (#1959). A chain's key is its file and the symbol or the reviewer's `family` (`sameSpot` and `familyLink`, #2048), and a family also keys across files: one family on two test files, or on any two files a reviewer's `repeatOf` links (`sameFamily`, `explicitLink`, #2334); such a chain's record names its `files`, its corrective may write each and its test sweep or rewrite lists each, and every reader of the key (`chainFindings`, `chainMembers`, `batteryOf`, `chainFamily`, `replayChainEscalation`, `chainCosts`) goes through `findingChain` or the record, never a file comparison of its own; a new field on a finding that a chain or another host decision reads is added to the finding schema (`ReviewSchema` in `schemas.ts`), kept readable by the bench trace export (`scrubTrace`, which keeps `family` folded), read by `chainFindings` and covered in `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts` (its `FINDING_FIELDS` table names each finding field's place in the chain key and fails when a new field skips it), and the chain replays (`replayChainEscalation`, the R62 and R65 md-site cases of `tests/forecast-replay-corpus.test.ts`) gain its column. A reviewer's own `repeatOf` link also joins on one file when either finding names no symbol (`explicitLink`, #2098), and a chain on a test file is routed to the test sweep (`corrective: "test-sweep"`; code that runs or shows a battery asks `rewritesSymbol`, never `decision === "rewrite"` alone). A new join rule adds a case to `tests/finding-chain.test.ts` with a pair of findings that the older rule failed to join. A reviewer's link also joins a class and its member on one file (`containsSymbol` over `symbolPath`, #2137): a language's qualified-name spelling (a separator, a receiver or arity form) joins `symbolPath`, which `symbolKey` reads, and its row joins `CONTAINER_MEMBER` in `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts`, which fails when a supported language lacks a container/member row. Every finding kind that can repeat carries `family` (`missing` and `partial` as well as `correctness`, `FAMILY_REVIEW_RULE`), so a family join never depends on the kind. A finding that repeats one whose corrective changed no file records the `repeat_without_change` would-stop (`recordUnchangedRepeats`, called from `appendCorrections`, which every review and convergence corrective path goes through); a new path that opens such correctives without `appendCorrections` calls it too, with a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts`. A finding's family `sites` (#2343) reach a corrective only through `checkSites` and `sitesStep` (`src/core/verification/family-sites.ts`), called from `appendCorrections`; a new corrective path for review or convergence findings passes the stated sites through them too, records `chains/sites/<task>.json` (`recordSites`) and adds its case to `tests/workflow-scenario-finding-chain.test.ts`, and a site is checked as code only through `maskNonCode`, never a language rule of its own. A corrective that puts back what the previous corrective of the file removed records the `corrective_restores_removed` would-stop (`recordRestoredRemovals`, called after every task diff that carries a `patch`, in place and integrated, #2277); a new path that records a corrective's diff keeps its `patch` and calls it, and a new restore rule adds its pair of patches to `tests/finding-chain.test.ts`.
