A finding field a reviewer fills to say what its repro cannot show (`noRepro`) is read by every gate that repairs on a reproduction: `reviewGateCandidates`, `reviewGateDecision` and the converge stage's `convergenceReproCandidates` and `convergenceGateDecision` (`src/core/workflows/review-gate.ts`) take it through `reproEvidence`, and `GateFinding` records it so the replay corpus decides it again. A new path that repairs on a repro outcome reads it too and adds its row to the noRepro cases of `tests/gate-matrix.test.ts` (#2194). Whether a documentation finding marked `noRepro` repairs is decided by its file, not the kind its reviewer chose, only through `docFindingRepairs` (`src/core/verification/repro.ts`), which both gates apply through `gateAllows` (`doc_finding_unrepaired`, enforce by default, `reviewGateDocKeeps`, `convergenceGateDocKeeps`); a new gate that repairs on a repro applies it the same way and adds its row to the #2835 cells of `tests/gate-matrix.test.ts`. A documentation finding a gate keeps that `docFindingRepairs` would repair rides a correction the same gate starts for another finding (`reviewGateDocCarries`, `convergenceGateDocCarries`, live, #2868), and its repeat kept out only by a later round's severity floor records the would-stop too (`reviewGateDocRepeats`, `convergenceGateDocRepeats`); a new gate that opens correctives carries and records them the same way, with its cells beside the #2835 ones.
