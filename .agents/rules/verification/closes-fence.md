Where a fenced Markdown block ends is decided by one function, `closesFence` in `src/core/verification/doc-examples.ts`, called only by `fences`; every reader of a request's or a document's fenced blocks (documented examples, fixtures, supplied-lines sizing) goes through `fences`, never a fence scan of its own, and a diff block (`Fence.diff`) is a change that no example or fixture reader takes as a program's text (#2018).
