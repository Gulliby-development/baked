Which criteria the derivation audit recomputes is decided only by `auditShape` in `src/core/workflows/derivation-audit.ts` (`rows` for tables and mappings, `computed` for a column, offset, width or count of a literal input in `then`); `auditedCriteria`, the missing-entry problems and the analyzer note all read it. A new audited shape gets its case there, a case in `tests/workflow-scenario-derivation-computed-value.test.ts` or `tests/workflow-scenario-derivation-audit.test.ts`, and its line in [the derivation audit](../../../docs/workflows/runtime.md) (#2022).
